What this site does not do

  • No cookies and nothing stored in your browser

  • No embedded videos, maps or social media widgets

Loading this page therefore opens no connection to any outside server.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA, part of Microsoft. As with any web server, access data is unavoidably recorded there: IP address, time of access, the file requested, browser and operating system.

That data is handled solely by the host in order to deliver the site and keep it running. I have no access to it, I don't analyse it and I store none of it anywhere. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; the legitimate interest is the technical operation of the site.

This may involve a transfer of data to the United States. Microsoft is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Details are in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Links to social platforms

The links to Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and LinkedIn are ordinary links, not embedded content. Unless you click one, none of those platforms learns about your visit. Once you do click, that platform's own privacy terms apply.

The follower numbers

The numbers shown on the front page are fetched automatically at intervals and saved as a plain file on this server. That fetch happens on the server, not in your browser. Visiting this site creates no connection to Instagram, TikTok or YouTube.

Your rights

Since I process no personal data about you, requests for access, correction or deletion have nothing to act on. You still hold the rights under Art. 15 to 21 GDPR, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority (Art. 77 GDPR). For the access data recorded by the host, GitHub is the right place to turn.

Encryption

The site is served exclusively over an encrypted HTTPS connection.
